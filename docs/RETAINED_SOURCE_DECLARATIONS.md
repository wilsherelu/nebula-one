Exact Process and Flow payloads remain beside their authored projection and import pins. The snapshot of references retains each property/unit source payload and content hash. The TianGong [data guidance](https://www.tiangong.earth/en/methodology) asks users to review each version's source and licence. A free-use statement and an open record state do not establish blanket redistribution rights for other versions.

An absent record-level licence field does not mean reuse is forbidden. The [European Commission legal notice](https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en) permits reuse of EU-owned website content under CC-BY-4.0 unless a specific notice or third-party rights say otherwise, with attribution and changes indicated. Records naming JRC as owner and a JRC permanent URI provide relevant source evidence; applicability to the exact transformed CSVs must still be traced rather than assumed.

The three EF CSV transformation chains have been checked against the JRC reference package extraction/reordering outputs; see [data provenance](DATA_PROVENANCE.md) for hashes, the 25 exact indicator identities and local display-field enhancements. Remaining concrete source trace gaps are the 22 KB mixed TIDAS reference seed and the three selected legacy elementary metadata rows: hashes, selection and source fields are retained, but their complete original-file transformation/attribution chain is not reconstructed. Four Flow and two reference payloads retain the owner/source declarations above; absence of a licence field alone is not a release blocker. These are metadata and method references, not commercial inventories.

The public mapping runtime files retain the CC-BY-4.0 licence and Nebula attribution NOTICE, which records written confirmation for identifiers without emission factors and excludes rights in underlying databases. Authored steel structure is separately licensed application content. Public upload remains pending resolution of the concrete reference-data trace gaps above.
